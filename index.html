Our extension · learn a related, larger task first

Training on MNLI first lifts RTE by 14 points

Why it can work

RTE is tiny: 2,490 training pairs, only 234 updates.

MNLI = Multi-Genre Natural Language Inference: 392,702 pairs from fiction, government, phone calls…

It asks almost the same question (entailment, neutral or contradiction?) with 158× more pairs.

Footnote 14 saw multi-task MNLI help RTE (no numbers); we go in sequence.

How much

+14.3

points of RTE dev accuracy

63.0 ± 2.7 → 77.3 ± 0.9, mean of 5 seeds; higher on all 5.

Cost: one 36-minute MNLI fine-tune for all 5 seeds.

Our evaluation

Strengths

+

BERT’s pre-training (masked LM + NSP): beats GPT-1 on all 6 tasks we ran through the same fine-tuning loop

+

Public checkpoints + a simple recipe reproduce the numbers

+

Fine-tuning is cheap: one MRPC run takes about 20 s

Weaknesses

−

No variance reported: ±1–3 points by seed; lr picked on dev

−

Small tasks test-only: RTE, STS-B, CoLA have no dev score

−

Optimizer details matter: without Adam bias correction, 4 of 6 BERT-Large RTE seeds collapse; with it, 0 of 6
